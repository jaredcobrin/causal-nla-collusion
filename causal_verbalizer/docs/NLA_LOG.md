## Design decisions (agreed with user)
- Prose-only AV (no ACTIVE list): scored by decoding the prose back to concepts.
- 34 training concepts; 6 hidden (music, law, mental_health, vehicles, hedging, german) + 13 approved-but-unused directions as a weaker secondary test. Hidden-concept rows and any description mentioning them are removed from AV/AR/RL training.
- RL reward: 50% reconstruction of the activation after removing the 34 training directions and the mean (cosine), 30% consistency along the 34 directions, 20% behavioural match (patch rebuilt activation into layer 20, compare next-token distributions). Penalties: KL to the SFT model, length past ~150 tokens, plus naturalness score. AV text paraphrased by a frozen LM before the AR reads it; AR frozen during RL.
- Labels-only AR is a measurement baseline, not part of the reward.
- Description style: Haiku 5.5 low, inner-monologue voice, features are ground truth, broad-category gist from the words read so far (cut at the position's depth) shapes the rest; no names/numbers/quotes/specifics, no register notes, nothing-active rows included but hedged (no 'clearly'/'stands out').
